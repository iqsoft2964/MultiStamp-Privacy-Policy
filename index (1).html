MultiStamp

Published by IQ-Soft

MultiStamp is an Android app for stamped photo and video capture, Projects, customizable stamps, reports and exports, with optional Google Drive photo storage and Google Play Premium subscriptions.
Privacy Policy Support

Core features

Capture stamped photos and videos, organize work into Projects, customize stamp templates, and create reports and exports.

Optional Google Drive

MultiStamp can connect to your Google Drive for supported photo storage, reports, and export packs. The app uses Google's limited drive.file authorization for app-authorized files and folders.

Subscriptions

Premium subscriptions are handled through Google Play. MultiStamp does not collect your payment-card number or Google password.

Support

Email iqsoft.support@gmail.com.

MultiStamp is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is planned for worldwide availability.
