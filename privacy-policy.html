1. About this policy

MultiStamp is an Android application that helps you capture stamped photos and videos, organize captures into Projects, customize stamps, and create reports and exports. This Privacy Policy explains the information used by those features and the choices available to you.

Core capture, stamping, and report creation take place on your device. Cloud-folder storage is optional. Google Play handles subscriptions. MultiStamp does not operate an IQ-Soft/MultiStamp server that stores or relays your captured media or cloud-folder files. This does not mean every feature is offline: Google services, your selected storage provider, and recipients you choose can receive information as described below.

You do not create a separate MultiStamp account to use the app. Google Play and a storage provider you select may use their own accounts. Selecting a folder does not create an IQ-Soft account or sign you into a MultiStamp account.

2. Photos, videos, audio, and information you enter

MultiStamp uses the camera when you choose to capture a photo or video. Microphone use is optional for video recording and requires permission after you choose Mic On. The microphone is off by default for a camera session. Recording is not designed to continue as background camera or microphone capture after you leave the foreground recording experience.

The app processes media and related information needed to apply stamps and organize your work. This can include capture date and time, Project names, notes, tags, template settings, selected location or address information, file references, dimensions, duration, and other capture metadata. The details included depend on the features and stamp fields you use.

Captured videos are stored locally by the app. The current app does not provide app-managed cloud-folder video uploads or cloud-only video retention. You can explicitly share a video through Android. Reports or export packs can include selected video thumbnails and metadata rather than the full video.

Information visibly burned into a photo or video, or included in an exported report, becomes part of that output. Review stamps and report contents before sending them to anyone else.

3. Location and address lookup

With your Android permission, MultiStamp uses approximate or precise foreground location for capture metadata and enabled stamp fields. It can process coordinates, location accuracy, location time, and altitude where available. The current app does not request Android background-location permission or implement background location tracking.

When address lookup is enabled, the app passes coordinates to Android's Geocoder service to obtain address suggestions. Depending on the device and service implementation, that lookup can use a network service. Coordinates and the request may therefore be processed by the location or geocoding provider even when your photo storage mode is Device only. MultiStamp does not route these lookups through an IQ-Soft content server.

You can manage location permission in Android settings and control the location or address fields you use in stamps. Removing permission or changing a stamp does not erase location information already burned into existing media or already exported.

4. Optional cloud-folder storage

Cloud-folder storage is optional. To enable it, you select a folder through Android’s system file picker. Google Drive may be available through its Android document provider; other compatible document providers may also appear. Availability and supported folder operations depend on your device and the selected provider. A folder offered by the picker can also be on local or removable storage, rather than a remote cloud service.

Android grants MultiStamp access to the selected folder and its contents as supported by the provider. MultiStamp keeps the folder reference, provider information, and persisted read/write access needed for continued use. It also keeps local Project, file, and transfer references. MultiStamp can display provider and folder labels, but does not obtain a verified connected-account email address through this folder-selection mechanism. Manage and verify the provider’s account within the provider’s own app or controls.

The selected provider receives the supported files and metadata that MultiStamp writes to the folder, including filenames and any Project details, notes, timestamps, location, or other information included in those files. The provider handles its own storage, account access, and any network transfer under its applicable terms, privacy practices, and your settings. MultiStamp does not relay these files through an IQ-Soft content server.

Photo storage supports three modes:

  • Device only: Photos remain on your device unless you explicitly share, export, or save a copy. This mode does not itself enable new automatic cloud-folder photo writes.
  • Device + cloud folder: After you select a folder and enable this mode, eligible photos can be written to that folder automatically while a local full-resolution copy is retained.
  • Cloud folder only: Each eligible photo is committed locally first. The app completes the destination write, independently reopens and reads the provider document, and verifies the expected content. Only after that verification may the local full-resolution copy be removed. Pending or failed transfers retain local full-resolution content. Small previews, metadata, and file or transfer references can remain locally.

Global and Project settings determine the applicable photo mode and destination. Supported photos, reports, and export packs can be organized within the selected folder by Project. After you select a folder and enable a supported cloud workflow, writes and retries may continue automatically, including through background transfer work. Selecting a provider folder is separate from explicitly sharing or exporting an output to another app or recipient.

Verification confirms that the expected content can be read back through the selected Android document provider. It does not prove that a remote cloud server has received, synchronized, or durably retained that content. The provider controls any remote synchronization, and MultiStamp does not guarantee it. When using Cloud folder only, keep a separate backup of important content and check the provider’s synchronization status before relying on a remote copy.

Opening, sharing, or using a cloud-retained photo in an output may require reading it again through the provider and creating a temporary private local copy. Temporary copies are managed as cache, not as a guaranteed permanent offline backup. Access can depend on the provider being available and on retaining or reselecting the appropriate folder.

MultiStamp does not itself make the selected folder public or create public sharing links as part of normal storage or Android sharing. The provider’s existing folder permissions, inherited access, or sharing settings can nevertheless allow others to access its contents. Review those settings before storing sensitive information.

Changing the folder does not move or delete files already written. Projects and transfers bound to the previous folder can remain bound to it and may require that folder to be reselected. Disconnecting pauses MultiStamp’s transfers for the destination, clears its active folder connection, and attempts to release its persisted access. Files already written are not deleted; pending content stays local. Known Project, file, and transfer references may remain for local history and safe reconnection. Disconnecting MultiStamp does not cancel synchronization already being performed by the provider.

5. Reports, exports, and sharing

Reports and export packs are generated using the captures and information you select. They may reproduce photos, thumbnails, notes, tags, Project details, timestamps, and location information.

When you choose Share or Save a Copy, the selected output is provided to the Android app, document provider, or other destination you choose. That recipient's privacy practices and access controls apply to its copy. MultiStamp cannot recall copies that recipients have saved or forwarded.

6. Google Play subscriptions and Welcome Access

Google Play handles purchase checkout and subscription payments. MultiStamp receives purchase and subscription-status information needed to acknowledge purchases and determine Premium access. The app does not collect your payment-card number, bank credentials, or Google password.

The app processes purchase tokens as needed through Play Billing without persisting purchase tokens or order identifiers in its own entitlement store or sending them to an IQ-Soft entitlement backend. A local entitlement record supports feature access and limited offline continuity.

MultiStamp also keeps a one-time Welcome Access redemption and timing record. That specific record is eligible for Android system cloud backup and device transfer, depending on your device settings and Android's backup operation. This is separate from optional cloud-folder photo storage and is not a general backup of your Projects, photos, or reports.

IQ-Soft may access Google Play order, refund, or related administrative information when needed for subscription administration, customer support and refunds, accounting, fraud prevention, or legal obligations. Such records are kept only as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes or as required by law.

Manage subscription cancellation and payment information through Google Play. Removing the app, clearing local data, or disconnecting a cloud folder is not a subscription-cancellation request.

6A. Google Play Billing diagnostics and your choice

Google Play handles subscriptions. Before MultiStamp starts its Google Play Billing connection, the app asks whether you want to enable Billing. When you enable it, Google’s Billing library may process and send technical diagnostics, including Billing API use and outcomes, connection and error results, app and device software information, and network or locale information. These diagnostics help provide and improve Billing reliability. Google’s library can queue them on your device and upload them later, including in the background.

Billing diagnostics are independent of optional cloud-folder storage and can operate when cloud-folder storage is off. You can use MultiStamp without enabling Billing, but purchase, price-loading, and restore connectivity will not be available through MultiStamp while Billing is disabled. You can change this choice later in Settings.

Turning Billing off stops MultiStamp from creating or reconnecting its Billing client. It does not cancel a Google Play subscription and does not mean that diagnostics already queued by Google’s library will be erased or prevented from uploading. MultiStamp cannot erase diagnostics already held by Google; Google’s applicable privacy practices and controls govern that information.

7. Retention, deletion, and your controls

Local media, Projects, and app records remain according to the storage mode and actions you choose. Local content, cloud files, and app history are separate copies or records; deleting one does not necessarily delete the others. Review the deletion choice shown by the app before confirming it.

You can manage app permissions through Android, change supported storage settings, disconnect a cloud folder, and use the app’s applicable deletion controls. You can also manage files directly through the selected provider. Where MultiStamp offers deletion of a known provider file or Project folder, it sends the deletion request through that provider. This is not a promise that the file will go to a recoverable trash folder: a provider may delete it permanently. Provider synchronization, trash, recovery, and any remaining retention follow that provider’s implementation, controls, and policies. Review the confirmation and keep any copy you need before deleting.

Uninstalling or clearing app data is not a reliable way to remove copies previously saved to shared device media storage, a selected document provider, or recipients. A Welcome Access record may be restored through Android backup or device transfer. Manage those external copies and backup settings separately.

IQ-Soft cannot remotely inspect or erase files kept solely on your device or in your selected provider’s storage. For privacy questions about information you separately provide to us, use the contact below. Do not send passwords, credentials, purchase tokens, or sensitive media unless specifically necessary for a support request.

7A. Request deletion of your data

This deletion-request process is for MultiStamp: Timestamp Camera (MultiStamp), published by IQ-Soft. You do not need to create a MultiStamp account or reinstall the app to make a request.

How to request deletion

  1. Email iqsoft.support@gmail.com with the subject MultiStamp data deletion request.
  2. Describe the information you want deleted, such as previous support messages or attachments, and give enough detail to locate it, such as the email address used and approximate date of contact. If possible, send the request from the address used to contact us. If you no longer have access to that address, explain this in your request.
  3. We may ask for the minimum information needed to verify that the data belongs to you. Do not send passwords, access codes, full payment details, or unnecessary sensitive media.

What we delete and when

We delete the requested support correspondence and attachments held by IQ-Soft, including your email address and other personal information you provided in those records, except for the limited retention cases below. We complete deletion within 60 days after verifying your request, or sooner where applicable law requires, and reply with the outcome.

Information that may be retained longer

Limited support, purchase-administration, order, or refund records may be retained where needed to complete an outstanding refund, meet accounting or legal obligations, or prevent fraud or abuse. Refund-related records are retained until the matter is resolved unless another stated retention reason applies. Accounting or legally required records are retained for the applicable required period. Fraud- or abuse-prevention records are retained while the specific prevention need continues. We will explain what is retained, why, and the applicable period or the criteria determining that period in our response.

Files and records held elsewhere

IQ-Soft cannot remotely erase photos, videos, Projects, reports, or other files kept solely on your device or in your selected storage provider. Use the app's applicable deletion controls, Android storage controls, or the provider's own controls for those copies. Manage Android backup copies separately. Copies already sent to other people must be managed with those recipients.

Google Play purchase records and Billing diagnostics held independently by Google follow Google's privacy practices and controls. A request to IQ-Soft does not erase those independently held records. Deleting data or uninstalling MultiStamp does not cancel a subscription; manage cancellation through Google Play.

8. Data collection, advertising, sale, and AI training

IQ-Soft does not sell personal data.

IQ-Soft does not use your MultiStamp photos, videos, location information, or cloud-folder content for advertising or AI-model training.

The current app has no in-app advertisements. MultiStamp does not operate an IQ-Soft entitlement or content backend that receives your captured media or cloud-folder files.

Data can still be processed by Google, Android, your device, document providers, or recipients you choose as necessary for the features described in this policy.

9. Security and service providers

MultiStamp uses Android storage and permission controls, persisted Android access grants for selected folders, and temporary Android access grants for supported sharing. For cloud-folder operations, MultiStamp exchanges files through the selected document provider. Any network transport, remote storage security, and encryption provided by that provider are controlled by the provider; MultiStamp does not promise a particular encryption method or remote-storage guarantee for every compatible provider.

No security measure provides an absolute guarantee. Device security, your provider and Google Play accounts, destination permissions, and recipients also affect protection of your information.

Services that may process information for the features described in this policy include your selected document provider (which may be Google Drive), Google Play and its Billing library, Android location or geocoding services, Android backup, and Gmail for support. Their own terms and privacy notices also apply. GitHub Pages hosting is described separately below.
