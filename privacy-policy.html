MultiStamp Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 7, 2026
Publisher: IQ-Soft
Application: MultiStamp
Android package: com.aistudio.multistamp
Privacy contact: iqsoft.support@gmail.com

Privacy summary: MultiStamp performs core capture, stamping, and report creation on your device. Google Drive storage is optional. Google Play handles subscriptions. IQ-Soft does not sell personal data and does not use your captured media, location information, or Google Drive data for advertising or AI-model training.

1. About this policy

MultiStamp is an Android application that helps you capture stamped photos and videos, organize captures into Projects, customize stamps, and create reports and exports. This Privacy Policy explains the information used by those features and the choices available to you.

Core capture, stamping, and report creation take place on your device. Google Drive storage is optional. Google Play handles subscriptions. MultiStamp does not operate an IQ-Soft/MultiStamp server that stores or relays your captured media or Google Drive files. This does not mean every feature is offline: Google services and recipients you choose can receive information as described below.

You do not create a separate MultiStamp account to use the app. Connecting Google Drive authorizes access to your existing Google account for optional Drive features; it does not create an IQ-Soft account.

2. Photos, videos, audio, and information you enter

MultiStamp uses the camera when you choose to capture a photo or video. Microphone use is optional for video recording and requires permission after you choose Mic On. The microphone is off by default for a camera session. Recording is not designed to continue as background camera or microphone capture after you leave the foreground recording experience.

The app processes media and related information needed to apply stamps and organize your work. This can include capture date and time, Project names, notes, tags, template settings, selected location or address information, file references, dimensions, duration, and other capture metadata. The details included depend on the features and stamp fields you use.

Captured videos are stored locally by the app. The current app does not provide app-managed Google Drive video uploads or cloud-only video retention. You can explicitly share a video through Android. Reports or export packs can include selected video thumbnails and metadata rather than the full video.

Information visibly burned into a photo or video, or included in an exported report, becomes part of that output. Review stamps and report contents before sending them to anyone else.

3. Location and address lookup

With your Android permission, MultiStamp uses approximate or precise foreground location for capture metadata and enabled stamp fields. It can process coordinates, location accuracy, location time, and altitude where available. The current app does not request Android background-location permission or implement background location tracking.

When address lookup is enabled, the app passes coordinates to Android's Geocoder service to obtain address suggestions. Depending on the device and service implementation, that lookup can use a network service. Coordinates and the request may therefore be processed by the location or geocoding provider even when your photo storage mode is Device only. MultiStamp does not route these lookups through an IQ-Soft content server.

You can manage location permission in Android settings and control the location or address fields you use in stamps. Removing permission or changing a stamp does not erase location information already burned into existing media or already exported.

4. Optional Google Drive storage

When you connect Google Drive, MultiStamp receives account information supplied by Google, including an account identifier, display name, and email address. It stores account and destination references locally to display the connected account, bind Projects to the correct account, and manage transfers.

The app requests Google's limited drive.file authorization for app-authorized files and folders rather than general access to every file in your Drive. It uses that access to create destinations, upload supported content, verify uploaded copies, retrieve known cloud-retained photos when you open or share them, and perform explicit supported remote-deletion actions.

Supported photos, reports, and export packs transfer directly between your device and Google Drive. Their contents and filenames may include the Project, notes, timestamps, location, or other information you chose to include. Google processes these transfers and stored files under its applicable policies and your account settings.

Photo storage supports Device only, Device + Google Drive, and Google Drive only retention. Global and Project settings determine the applicable mode. After you enable a cloud photo mode, eligible photos can be queued and uploaded automatically under that choice, including background transfer or retry work. Device only does not itself enable automatic photo upload.

Every cloud-mode photo is committed locally first. In Google Drive only retention, local full-resolution data is removed only after the app verifies the Drive copy. Pending or failed uploads keep the local full-resolution data. A small local preview, metadata, and transfer references can remain after full-resolution data is retained in Drive.

Opening or sharing a cloud-retained photo may download a temporary private copy. Temporary copies are managed as cache, not as a guaranteed permanent offline backup.

Normal storage and Share do not create a public Drive link. However, access you grant in Google Drive, including access inherited from a destination, can affect who can see its contents. Review destination access before storing sensitive information there.

Disconnecting Google Drive pauses app transfers and clears the active connection; it does not delete files already in Drive. Known Project or transfer references may remain for local history and safe reconnection.

5. Reports, exports, and sharing

Reports and export packs are generated using the captures and information you select. They may reproduce photos, thumbnails, notes, tags, Project details, timestamps, and location information.

When you choose Share or Save a Copy, the selected output is provided to the Android app, document provider, destination, or Google Drive account you choose. That recipient's privacy practices and access controls apply to its copy. MultiStamp cannot recall copies that recipients have saved or forwarded.

6. Google Play subscriptions and Welcome Access

Google Play handles purchase checkout and subscription payments. MultiStamp receives purchase and subscription-status information needed to acknowledge purchases and determine Premium access. The app does not collect your payment-card number, bank credentials, or Google password.

The app processes purchase tokens as needed through Play Billing without persisting purchase tokens or order identifiers in its own entitlement store or sending them to an IQ-Soft entitlement backend. A local entitlement record supports feature access and limited offline continuity.

MultiStamp also keeps a one-time Welcome Access redemption and timing record. That specific record is eligible for Android system cloud backup and device transfer, depending on your device settings and Android's backup operation. This is separate from optional Google Drive photo storage and is not a general backup of your Projects, photos, or reports.

IQ-Soft may access Google Play order, refund, or related administrative information when needed for subscription administration, customer support and refunds, accounting, fraud prevention, or legal obligations. Such records are kept only as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes or as required by law.

Manage subscription cancellation and payment information through Google Play. Removing the app, clearing local data, or disconnecting Drive is not a subscription-cancellation request.

7. Retention, deletion, and your controls

Local media, Projects, and app records remain according to the storage mode and actions you choose. Local content, cloud files, and app history are separate copies or records; deleting one does not necessarily delete the others. Review the deletion choice shown by the app before confirming it.

You can manage app permissions through Android, change supported storage settings, disconnect Google Drive, and use the app's applicable deletion controls. You can also manage remote files directly in Google Drive. Supported app remote-deletion actions move known Drive objects to trash; permanent deletion and any remaining provider retention follow Google's controls and policies.

Uninstalling or clearing app data is not a reliable way to remove copies previously saved to shared device media storage, Google Drive, other document providers, or recipients. A Welcome Access record may be restored through Android backup or device transfer. Manage those external copies and backup settings separately.

IQ-Soft cannot remotely inspect or erase files kept solely on your device or in your personal Google Drive. For privacy questions about information you separately provide to us, use the contact below. Do not send passwords, credentials, purchase tokens, or sensitive media unless specifically necessary for a support request.

8. Data collection, advertising, sale, and AI training

IQ-Soft does not sell personal data.

IQ-Soft does not use your MultiStamp photos, videos, location information, or Google Drive data for advertising or AI-model training.

The current app has no in-app advertisements. MultiStamp does not operate an IQ-Soft entitlement or content backend that receives your captured media or Google Drive files.

Data can still be processed by Google, Android, your device, document providers, or recipients you choose as necessary for the features described in this policy.

9. Security and service providers

MultiStamp uses Android storage and permission controls, HTTPS connections for its direct Google Drive transfers, and temporary Android access grants for supported sharing. Access tokens are held in process memory. Protected resumable-upload session information is stored using Android Keystore-backed encryption.

No security measure provides an absolute guarantee. Device security, your Google account, destination permissions, and recipients also affect protection of your information.

Google authorization, Google Drive, Google Play, Android location or geocoding, Android backup, and Gmail support services may process information needed for their functions. Their own terms and privacy notices also apply.

10. Support and contact information

Support is handled directly by the app owner through Gmail at iqsoft.support@gmail.com. When you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any information or attachments you choose to send so that we can respond to your request.

Support emails and related records are kept only as long as reasonably necessary to resolve the request, maintain appropriate support records, prevent abuse, or meet legal obligations, and may be deleted when they are no longer needed.

For privacy questions or requests concerning information you have directly provided to IQ-Soft, contact: iqsoft.support@gmail.com

11. Adults only

MultiStamp is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. If you are under 18, do not use MultiStamp.

12. Worldwide availability and local rights

MultiStamp is intended for worldwide distribution.

Depending on where you live, applicable privacy or consumer-protection law may provide rights relating to personal information you directly provide to IQ-Soft, such as support correspondence or purchase-administration records. You may contact us using the privacy email below. Rights relating to information held by Google or another service provider should be exercised through that provider's controls where applicable.

13. Changes to this policy

IQ-Soft may update this policy when MultiStamp's functionality, permissions, software components, data practices, or legal obligations change. The effective or last-updated date will be revised when material changes are published. If a future version changes how personal information is processed, the policy and applicable in-app or platform disclosures will be updated as required.
